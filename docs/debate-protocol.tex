\bptitleblock{Debate protocol}{``Robust enough'' is a decision, not a number — run it yourselves in 20 minutes}{DOCUMENT NNV-DP \quad|\quad REV A \quad|\quad 16 September 2026}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{0:00–2:00} & \textbf{Read.} Pick one scenario card (medical triage / authentication / autonomous control). Everyone reads the facts box. Deal the four role cards; if you are three, the verification engineer's card is shared.\\
\textbf{2:00–10:00} & \textbf{In role.} Each role states its goal in one sentence (1 minute round). Then argue the decision sheet line by line, starting with \emph{the property} and \emph{the region}. Stay in role; use the ``uncomfortable question'' on your card at least once.\\
\textbf{10:00–15:00} & \textbf{Converge.} Out of role. Fill every line of the decision sheet. Blank lines are allowed only if you write \emph{why} you could not agree.\\
\textbf{15:00–20:00} & \textbf{Challenge.} Swap sheets with a neighbouring table. Read theirs; challenge exactly one line in writing; hand it back.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section*{Ground rules}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A number without a region is not an answer (``99\,\% accurate'' — on which inputs?).
  \item A proof without its assumptions is not an answer (``proved safe'' — for which region, under which model of the world?).
  \item ``We will test it more'' is not an answer to a counterexample the verifier found in a second.
  \item Every line of the sheet has an owner. Write the role, not a name.
\end{itemize}

\section*{If you get stuck}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{What is the region?} Name the inputs, their ranges and units. If you cannot, the debate is about that.
  \item \emph{What happens the first time the verifier reports a counterexample (\badmark) in production?} Who is told, in what time, and what is switched off?
  \item \emph{Who wrote the sentence, and who checked it against the world?} The San Antonio recall was a specification, not a perception failure.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{notebox}
\textbf{Afterwards.} Take the sheet to your own project with the worksheet \emph{Specify your system} (NNV-WS): one requirement as a sentence about every input in a region, what a counterexample would look like, and what cannot be verified this way.
\end{notebox}
